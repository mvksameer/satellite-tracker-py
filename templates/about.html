About the project

Advanced Satellite Tracker downloads current orbital data from CelesTrak and works out exactly when a satellite will pass over a chosen location. Skyfield handles the astronomical calculations, and each pass is drawn out as a plot you can read at a glance.

Live orbital data

Orbital elements refresh automatically from CelesTrak, so every calculation reflects a satellite's current path rather than an outdated one.

Five views of a pass

Choose elevation, azimuth, distance and velocity, a polar sky track, or a ground track, depending on what you want to see.

Automatic pass detection

Rise time, set time, maximum elevation and duration are worked out for you, with the clearest passes highlighted.

Any observer location

Enter a latitude and longitude anywhere on Earth to see passes tailored to that spot.

Under the bonnet, Skyfield propagates each satellite's two-line element set with the SGP4 model, converts the result into altitude, azimuth and distance from the observer, and Matplotlib renders the outcome as a chart.
